# About Dr. Bashir Ahmad

**Dr. Bashir Ahmad** is an Associate Professor in the Department of Biological Sciences and serves as the Director of the Directorate of Graduate Studies (DGS) at the International Islamic University Islamabad. He completed his doctoral degree in Microbiology jointly between Quaid-i-Azam University, Islamabad and the **University of Waterloo, Canada**, followed by post-doctoral research at the renowned **Institute of Biotechnology, University of Cambridge, UK**.

Dr. Ahmad directs the **Applied Microbiology and Biotechnology Laboratory (AMBL)** at IIUI. His research focuses on the intersection of microbial physiology, industrial fermentation, and **biotechnology commercialization**. His laboratory isolates extremophilic and indigenous bacteria to produce industrially relevant enzymes (such as cellulases, proteases, and lipases) for the textile, detergent, and biofuels industries.

Recognized as a champion of university-industry linkages, Dr. Ahmad has successfully secured competitive grants under the **HEC Technology Development Fund (TDF)**, developing prototypes and pilot-scale fermentation processes for commercialization. He has supervised over **40 graduate students** and advises national bodies on biotech entrepreneurship and incubation.
