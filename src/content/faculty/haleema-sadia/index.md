# About Prof. Dr. Haleema Sadia

**Prof. Dr. Haleema Sadia** is Professor in the Department of Biological Sciences at IIUI. She completed her doctoral research in collaboration with the **University of Cambridge, UK**. Dr. Sadia focuses on **cancer genomics**, **precision oncology**, and **congenital diseases**, using Next-Generation Sequencing (NGS) to discover genetic biomarkers in Pakistani cohorts.
