Dr. Bushra Uzair Zafar is a Tenured Associate Professor at the Department of Biological Sciences, International Islamic University Islamabad. Holding a Ph.D. in **Microbiology and Biotechnology**, her research concentrates on the urgent global challenge of **antimicrobial resistance (AMR)** and the discovery of novel bioactive therapeutic compounds from extreme natural habitats.

Her laboratory investigates **bacterial virulence factors, biofilm formation, and plasmid-mediated multidrug resistance pathways**. Through collaborative partnerships with national hospitals and pharmaceutical institutes, Dr. Bushra leads projects characterizing therapeutic efficacy of natural metabolites against resistant clinical isolates.
