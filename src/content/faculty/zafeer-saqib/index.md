Prof. Dr. Zafeer Saqib is a Professor and Academic Lead at the Department of Biological Sciences, Faculty of Sciences, International Islamic University Islamabad (IIUI). With a doctoral degree specializing in **Environmental Biology and Landscape Ecology**, his pioneering research focuses on the intersection of biological diversity, geospatial technologies, ecological modeling, and habitat conservation.

Dr. Saqib leads advanced research programs utilizing **remote sensing, GIS, and computational ecology** to quantify habitat fragmentation, vegetation dynamics, and anthropogenic pressures on vulnerable ecosystems across Pakistan and South Asia. He actively mentors graduate students in **computational biology, biostatistics, and environmental biosciences**, fostering interdisciplinary collaboration between molecular biology and macro-ecological monitoring.
