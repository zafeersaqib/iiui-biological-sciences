# About Dr. Nyla Jabeen

**Dr. Nyla Jabeen** is Associate Professor and Founding Head of the Applied Biotechnology and Genetic Engineering (ABGE) Laboratory. She completed post-doctoral research at **Cornell University, USA**. Her laboratory specializes in **CRISPR-Cas9 genome editing**, **agricultural biotechnology**, and **molecular farming**.
