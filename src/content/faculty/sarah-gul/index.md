Dr. Sarah Gul is a Tenured Associate Professor at the Department of Biological Sciences, Faculty of Sciences, International Islamic University Islamabad. Holding a Ph.D. in **Biological Sciences**, her research investigates the molecular mechanisms governing gene regulation, aberrant cellular signaling pathways, and pathological biomarker identification in non-communicable diseases.

Her current projects integrate cell biology, proteomics, and industrial biotechnology applications, assessing natural bio-modulators for therapeutic and metabolic intervention.
