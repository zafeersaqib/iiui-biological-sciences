# About Dr. Shaheen Shahzad

**Dr. Shaheen Shahzad** is an Associate Professor and Program Incharge in the Department of Biological Sciences at the International Islamic University Islamabad (IIUI). She earned her Ph.D. in Molecular Genetics and Biochemistry from Quaid-i-Azam University, Islamabad, and is an **HEC-Approved Doctoral Supervisor**.

Dr. Shahzad heads the **Genomics Research Laboratory** at IIUI, where her team investigates the genetic basis of **complex multifactorial diseases** including Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus, cardiovascular disease, coronary artery pathology, and inherited metabolic syndromes in Pakistani cohorts. Her research combines **Genome-Wide Association Studies (GWAS)**, targeted Sanger sequencing, real-time quantitative PCR (RT-qPCR), and bioinformatic functional prediction tools.

Her research portfolio includes over **35 peer-reviewed publications** in international indexing journals, and she has supervised numerous doctoral and master's candidates to graduation. Dr. Shahzad also leads departmental curriculum alignment initiatives to ensure undergraduate and graduate degree structures adhere strictly to modern HEC standards.
