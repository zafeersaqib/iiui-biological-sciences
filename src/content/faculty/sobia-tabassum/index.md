# About Prof. Dr. Sobia Tabassum

**Prof. Dr. Sobia Tabassum** is a Tenured Professor in the Department of Biological Sciences at IIUI. She completed post-doctoral training at the prestigious **University of Oxford, UK** and her Ph.D. jointly between Quaid-i-Azam University and **Washington State University, USA**. She serves as the official Ambassador for the European Association for Cancer Research (EACR) and directs the Pharmaceutical Biotechnology Laboratory at IIUI.
