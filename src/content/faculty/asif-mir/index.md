# About Prof. Dr. Asif Mir

**Prof. Dr. Asif Mir** is Tenured Professor of Molecular Biology and Biochemistry in the Department of Biological Sciences at IIUI. An HEC-approved doctoral supervisor, Dr. Mir has contributed extensively to **molecular genetics**, **biochemical assays**, and **protein characterization**. He has trained numerous MS and PhD candidates and served in key departmental leadership roles.
