# About Prof. Dr. Asma Gul

**Prof. Dr. Asma Gul** is Tenured Professor and Chairperson of the Department of Biological Sciences at the International Islamic University Islamabad (IIUI). Following her doctoral training at Quaid-i-Azam University, Islamabad, she completed a prestigious Post-Doctoral Fellowship at the **University of Cambridge, United Kingdom**, focusing on advanced molecular genetics and functional genomics.

Dr. Gul's research laboratory investigates the molecular etiology of **inherited human genetic disorders**, **familial cancer syndromes**, and **somatic mutations in solid tumors** within consanguineous Pakistani cohorts. Her group integrates **Next-Generation DNA Sequencing (NGS)**, bioinformatics pipelines, and molecular functional assays to discover novel pathogenic variants and characterize regulatory non-coding variants.

In addition to genomic research, Dr. Gul leads pioneering cross-disciplinary investigations in **nanomedicine**, designing functionalized nanoparticle matrices for targeted delivery of chemotherapeutic agents and botanical bioactive molecules. She also explores **psychoneuroimmunology**, examining epigenetic and cytokine biomarkers linked with psychological stress and immune dysregulation.

She has supervised more than **15 Ph.D. scholars** and over **60 MS researchers**, securing multiple competitive research grants from the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan and the Pakistan Science Foundation (PSF). Dr. Gul is an HEC-Approved Doctoral Supervisor and actively serves on national scientific advisory panels.
