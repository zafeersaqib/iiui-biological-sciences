# About Dr. M. Imran Shabbir

**Dr. M. Imran Shabbir** is Tenured Associate Professor in the Department of Biological Sciences. He earned his Ph.D. from the prestigious **CEMB Lahore** followed by a Post-Doctoral Fellowship at the **University of California, Santa Cruz (UCSC), USA**. His lab explores molecular genetics, high-throughput sequencing, and optical biosensor development.
