Dr. Naveeda Riaz is a Tenured Professor at the Department of Biological Sciences, International Islamic University Islamabad. She earned her Ph.D. from the Centre of Excellence in Molecular Biology (CEMB) and completed a five-year Postdoctoral Fellowship at the **National Institutes of Health (NIH), Bethesda, Maryland, USA** (2001–2006).

Her research program focuses on **human molecular genetics**, particularly uncovering novel genetic mutations responsible for congenital hearing loss, inherited syndromic disorders, and complex developmental anomalies in consanguineous Pakistani pedigrees. She is an author on numerous high-impact international genetics publications and oversees departmental bioethics curriculum.
