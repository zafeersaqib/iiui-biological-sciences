# Bachelor of Science in Biology (BSBIO)

The **BS Biology** program at the International Islamic University Islamabad is a four-year interdisciplinary degree formulated to equip students with sound theoretical mastery and laboratory competencies across biological sciences.

## Academic Philosophy
Biological discovery is experiencing an epochal revolution through genomics, digital biosensing, and environmental modeling. At IIUI, our pedagogy unites foundational biological concepts with high-throughput laboratory skills, empowering graduates to address urgent ecological, agricultural, and clinical challenges facing Pakistan and the world.
