# IIUI Biological Sciences Convenes National Symposium on Human Genomics

The Department of Biological Sciences at the International Islamic University Islamabad hosted the **National Symposium on Human Genomics and Precision Healthcare**, bringing together researchers from national universities, clinical diagnostic institutes, and biotechnology companies.

Keynotes addressed the emerging role of **Next-Generation DNA Sequencing (NGS)** in resolving rare inherited disorders in consanguineous families, pharmacogenomics in cardiovascular therapy, and population-level genetic risk scoring in South Asia.

Over 200 undergraduate and graduate students presented posters detailing their original empirical discoveries, with outstanding awards distributed by university leadership.
