# Admissions Open for Undergraduate and Postgraduate Bioscience Degrees

The International Islamic University Islamabad announces the opening of online admission applications for the **Department of Biological Sciences, Faculty of Sciences**.

Eligible candidates seeking admission to **BS Biology**, **BS Biotechnology**, **MS Biotechnology**, and **Ph.D. Biotechnology** are invited to submit applications through the official IIUI Admissions Portal at [admission.iiu.edu.pk](https://admission.iiu.edu.pk/).

All programs feature state-of-the-art laboratory practical sessions, experienced Ph.D. faculty mentorship, and comprehensive curricula aligned with Higher Education Commission (HEC) standards.
