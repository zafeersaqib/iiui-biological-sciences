# Department Secures Multi-Million HEC Technology Development Fund Grant

The Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan has officially approved competitive grant funding under the **Technology Development Fund (TDF)** to the Department of Biological Sciences, Faculty of Sciences, International Islamic University Islamabad.

The project, titled **"Pilot-Scale Production and Commercialization of Indigenous Industrial Enzymes for the Textile and Detergent Sectors"**, is headed by **Dr. Bashir Ahmad** as Principal Investigator in collaboration with national industrial manufacturing partners.

## Strategic Impact
This initiative aims to substitute expensive imported enzymatic formulations utilized in textile desizing and denim processing with high-efficiency biocatalysts produced using locally isolated microbial strains. The research will be hosted in the Applied Microbiology and Biotechnology Laboratory (AMBL) and will provide advanced stipend-supported training for graduate researchers.

Speaking on the milestone, Chairperson **Prof. Dr. Asma Gul** remarked:
> "This achievement exemplifies our commitment to translational science that creates tangible value for Pakistan's industrial sectors while upholding the highest standards of bioscience innovation."
