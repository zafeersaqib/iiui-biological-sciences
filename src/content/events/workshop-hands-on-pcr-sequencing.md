# Hands-on Workshop: RT-qPCR and NGS Workflows

Join the Department of Biological Sciences for a comprehensive hands-on laboratory workshop covering the complete experimental workflow for quantitative gene expression analysis and modern DNA sequencing preparation.

## Key Modules
1. **Total RNA Isolation & Purity QC**: NanoDrop spectrophotometry and bioanalyzer assessment.
2. **Reverse Transcription & cDNA Synthesis**: High-fidelity reverse transcriptase protocols.
3. **Primer Design & Amplicon Optimization**: Using NCBI Primer-BLAST and OligoCalc.
4. **Real-Time PCR Execution**: Setting up amplification on the Bio-Rad CFX96 system and analyzing $\Delta\Delta C_T$ fold-change expression.
5. **NGS Library Prep Overview**: Tagmentation, adapter ligation, and QC parameters.
