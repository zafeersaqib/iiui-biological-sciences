# International Conference on Advances in Molecular Biosciences (ICAMBB)

The Faculty of Sciences and Department of Biological Sciences welcome submissions and registrations for the International Conference on Advances in Molecular Biosciences & Biotechnology.

## Conference Tracks
* **Track 1**: Human Molecular Genetics, Rare Diseases & Cancer Genomics
* **Track 2**: Microbial Biotechnology, Biocatalysts & Industrial Fermentation
* **Track 3**: Agricultural Biotechnology, Plant Stress Physiology & GMOs
* **Track 4**: Nanobiotechnology, Biosensors & Targeted Drug Delivery
* **Track 5**: Bioethics, Biosafety & Islamic Perspectives on Emerging Technologies
