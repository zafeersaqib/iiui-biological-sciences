## Overview
This research area addresses cancer predisposition mutations in breast, ovarian, and colorectal carcinomas within high-consanguinity Pakistani cohorts. In parallel, nanobiotechnology researchers develop smart biopolymeric drug-delivery scaffolds to enhance therapeutic efficacy.
