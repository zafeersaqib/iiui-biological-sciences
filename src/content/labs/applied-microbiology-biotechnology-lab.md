# Applied Microbiology & Biotechnology Laboratory (AMBL)

The **Applied Microbiology & Biotechnology Laboratory (AMBL)** serves as the department's core industrial bioprocess and fermentation research unit. The laboratory Bridges foundational microbial biology with pilot-scale technological applications.

## Key Industrial Capabilities
* **Bioprocess Optimization**: Statistical optimization (Plackett-Burman, Box-Behnken RSM) of growth substrates using local agricultural byproducts.
* **Biocatalyst Engineering**: Discovery and formulation of thermostable, alkali-tolerant amylases, proteases, and cellulases for industrial textile desizing and bio-detergents.
* **Technology Transfer**: Translating laboratory discoveries into viable industrial proof-of-concept prototypes under HEC-TDF funding.
