# Genomics & Molecular Genetics Research Laboratory

The **Genomics & Molecular Genetics Research Laboratory** is a specialized departmental facility dedicated to understanding the genetic foundations of hereditary diseases, cancer syndromes, and metabolic conditions prevalent in Pakistani populations.

## Research Focus Areas
1. **Targeted Exome & Variant Screening**: Investigating consanguineous cohorts to identify novel disease-associated sequence variants.
2. **Cardiometabolic Biomarker Discovery**: Characterizing genomic variants associated with premature coronary artery disease, familial hypercholesterolemia, and Type 2 Diabetes.
3. **Translational Genomics**: Integrating clinical phenotypic data with high-density genomic scans to formulate population-specific risk prediction algorithms.
